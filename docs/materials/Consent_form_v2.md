## What this study is about

We are studying how an AI practice partner should behave when people rehearse difficult conversations with an academic advisor. You will chat with "Dr. Lee", a fictional advisor played by an AI. At the end, a researcher will play Dr. Lee in one short spoken conversation.

## What you will do

- Answer short questionnaires about how you feel (STAI-6), how confident you feel, and how the conversations went.
- Have three short typed practice conversations with the AI, and one spoken conversation with a researcher.
- Rank the three versions of Dr. Lee and answer a few interview questions.
- The session takes about 60 to 70 minutes. There is a short break between parts.

## Your data

- Your answers and chat messages are saved under a participant ID, not your name. This signed form is stored separately from that ID.
- To produce Dr. Lee's replies, your typed messages are sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API). Please do not type real personal information. The situations are made up.
- The spoken conversation and the short interview are audio recorded, if you agree below. The recording is transcribed, and the transcript is saved under your participant ID. If you do not agree, the researcher takes written notes instead.
- Only the research team can see the data. Results are reported for the group or under the participant ID, never with your name.
- Data is kept until [retention period, e.g. the end of the course in December 2026] and then deleted. Signed forms are kept for [period].
- You can ask for your data to be deleted until [date], by contacting the team with your participant ID.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary.
- You may skip any question or any conversation, or stop at any time, without giving a reason and without any penalty. Just tell the researcher, or use "I want to stop this round" in the chat.
- If a conversation feels stressful, you can pause. The researcher will check in with you.
